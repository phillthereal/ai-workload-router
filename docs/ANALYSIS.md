# Analysis: confidence intervals and product questions

Generated by `analyze.py` from the logged, non-simulated benchmark runs (`data/runs.db`, opened read-only). Zero API calls. Regenerate with `python3 analyze.py`.

## 1. Bootstrap confidence intervals

Method: paired bootstrap. Within one run_group, whole tasks are resampled with replacement (router and frontier outcome for a task travel together), 10,000 resamples, seed 20261020, percentile 95% interval. Net cost reduction is net of `routing_cost_usd`, and quality retention is mean routed quality / mean frontier quality, exactly as in `src/router/report.py`. Every point estimate below equals the number published in `docs/V2_FINDINGS.md` / `docs/V3_FINDINGS.md` (checked to 0.1pp at generation time; the script aborts otherwise).

| Result | n | Net cost reduction % [95% CI] | Quality retention % [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|---|
| v1 cross_vendor labels, easy (n=25) | 25 | 53.6% [29.9, 82.6] | 100.4% [99.0, 102.0] |
| v2 cross_vendor classifier, easy (n=25) | 25 | 65.6% [40.3, 97.7] | 99.8% [97.8, 101.6] |
| v2 claude_tiers classifier, easy (n=25) | 25 | 42.5% [23.4, 66.8] | 100.3% [98.9, 101.9] |
| v2 claude_tiers cascade, easy (n=25) | 25 | 53.1% [25.1, 72.1] | 99.6% [98.0, 101.4] |
| v2 claude_tiers classifier, HARD (n=10) | 10 | -6.3% [-21.6, 6.7] | 100.0% [100.0, 100.0] |

**Reading the intervals.** With n=25, 10 and 14 tasks the intervals are wide; treat the point estimates as directional.

- Cost-reduction CI excludes 0: v1 cross_vendor labels, easy (n=25), v2 cross_vendor classifier, easy (n=25), v2 claude_tiers classifier, easy (n=25), v2 claude_tiers cascade, easy (n=25), v3 learned vs static router, held-out (n=14), cost diff / quality ratio.

## 2. Product questions answered in SQL

Scope: non-simulated rows (`simulated = 0`), no `live:%` strategies, and only run_groups that have a committed benchmark report (so cache-replay duplicates are not double counted). Results are pooled across those experiments, so read them as a cross-experiment overview, not one clean A/B. A temp view `v` holds those rows; `rg(run_group, label)` maps groups to experiments.

### Q1. Which task types should never be routed? (savings vs quality change, routed arm vs frontier)

```sql
SELECT e.task_type, COUNT(*) AS n,
  ROUND(SUM(b.cost_usd), 5) AS frontier_cost,
  ROUND(SUM(e.cost_usd + e.routing_cost_usd), 5) AS routed_cost_net,
  ROUND(100.0 * (1 - SUM(e.cost_usd + e.routing_cost_usd) / SUM(b.cost_usd)), 1) AS net_saving_pct,
  ROUND(AVG(b.quality_score), 3) AS q_frontier,
  ROUND(AVG(e.quality_score), 3) AS q_routed,
  ROUND(AVG(e.quality_score - b.quality_score), 3) AS q_change
FROM v e JOIN v b ON b.run_group = e.run_group AND b.task_id = e.task_id
 AND b.strategy = 'frontier_only'
WHERE e.strategy != 'frontier_only'
GROUP BY e.task_type ORDER BY q_change
```

| task_type | n | frontier_cost | routed_cost_net | net_saving_pct | q_frontier | q_routed | q_change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| classification | 66 | 0.30722 | 0.06138 | 80.0 | 0.995 | 0.95 | -0.046 |
| reasoning | 54 | 0.34308 | 0.27499 | 19.8 | 1.0 | 0.963 | -0.037 |
| short_generation | 43 | 0.12949 | 0.03027 | 76.6 | 0.979 | 0.958 | -0.021 |
| extraction | 65 | 0.11341 | 0.04103 | 63.8 | 1.0 | 0.985 | -0.015 |

**So what:** `classification` has the largest quality change (-0.046); `extraction` the smallest (-0.015) — compare against its saving before routing it.

### Q2. Where does routing lose money? (net cost delta by difficulty)

```sql
SELECT e.difficulty, COUNT(*) AS n,
  ROUND(SUM(b.cost_usd), 5) AS frontier_cost,
  ROUND(SUM(e.cost_usd + e.routing_cost_usd), 5) AS routed_cost_net,
  ROUND(SUM(b.cost_usd - e.cost_usd - e.routing_cost_usd), 5) AS net_saving_usd,
  SUM(e.cost_usd + e.routing_cost_usd > b.cost_usd) AS tasks_costlier_when_routed
FROM v e JOIN v b ON b.run_group = e.run_group AND b.task_id = e.task_id
 AND b.strategy = 'frontier_only'
WHERE e.strategy != 'frontier_only'
GROUP BY e.difficulty ORDER BY net_saving_usd
```

| difficulty | n | frontier_cost | routed_cost_net | net_saving_usd | tasks_costlier_when_routed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hard | 92 | 0.28689 | 0.18039 | 0.1065 | 24 |
| medium | 58 | 0.32683 | 0.1592 | 0.16763 | 4 |
| easy | 78 | 0.27948 | 0.06808 | 0.2114 | 9 |

**So what:** `hard` tasks net the least (0.1065 USD) and 24 of 92 routed runs cost more than the frontier baseline; a negative net_saving_usd would mean routing lost money overall.

### Q4. Is routing overhead worth it? (overhead as share of gross savings, per experiment)

```sql
SELECT e.label, COUNT(*) AS n,
  ROUND(SUM(b.cost_usd - e.cost_usd), 5) AS gross_saving_usd,
  ROUND(SUM(e.routing_cost_usd), 5) AS overhead_usd,
  ROUND(100.0 * SUM(e.routing_cost_usd) / NULLIF(SUM(b.cost_usd - e.cost_usd), 0), 1) AS overhead_pct_of_savings
FROM v e JOIN v b ON b.run_group = e.run_group AND b.task_id = e.task_id
 AND b.strategy = 'frontier_only'
WHERE e.strategy != 'frontier_only'
GROUP BY e.label ORDER BY overhead_pct_of_savings DESC
```

## 3. Limitations

- Small n (25 / 10 / 14 tasks) and one run per configuration: intervals are wide and there is no run-to-run variance estimate.
- Task-level resampling assumes these tasks are representative of real traffic; the task sets are self-authored, not sampled from production.
- Judge scores (Claude Opus 4.8 rubric judge, exact match elsewhere) are treated as ground truth; judge bias is not propagated into the intervals.
- Easy-set quality is near ceiling (about 1.0), so retention intervals are narrow partly because there is little room to vary.
- Pooled SQL tables mix experiments with different rosters and difficulty; use the per-experiment table (Q4) before drawing a conclusion about any single setup.
